Parents and guardians, register your athlete below. It takes about 10 minutes, and there's no cost to apply.

What happens next

  1. Your registration opens in your email, ready to send to Ava's Place.
  2. We review it and reach out to your family within about two weeks.
  3. We talk through goals and what support fits best.

Questions? carrie@avasplace.org
