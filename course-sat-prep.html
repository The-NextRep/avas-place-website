College readiness

SAT Prep

The SAT rewards calm pacing and fixing repeat mistakes more than cramming. This course applies the skills from the other Ava's Place courses to one high-stakes test on the road to college.

Launchers (grades 10–12) heading to college, plus adults re-entering school who need a score. Self-paced: 6 modules (short video, practice set, Error Log entry), about 60–90 minutes each. Learners move at their own speed, with a suggested pace of one module per week and an optional live Q&A office hour.

Start module 1 Print a Rep Card

Core tool

The Error Log

For every missed question: topic · why I missed it (didn't know, misread, rushed, ran out of time) · the fix · retest date.

Before you start: quick self-check

Rate each line from 1 (not yet) to 5 (definitely) before module 1, then again after your Real-World Rep.

The test at a glance

Fully digital in the Bluebook app. Two sections, Reading and Writing (64 min) then Math (70 min), each split into two adaptive modules. 98 questions, 2 hours 14 minutes of testing, 10-minute break, scored 400–1600. The Desmos calculator is available on all Math questions.

Free practice: official full-length practice tests in Bluebook and College Board's free Official Digital SAT Prep on Khan Academy. Families pay nothing extra.

The modules

6 modules, one per week.

Each module: a short video, a practice set or field task, and a log entry. Go at your own pace; one module a week works well.

  1. Module 1

    Know the Test

    Hook5–10 min

    Walk through Bluebook: modules, tools, timing, how adaptive works

    Rep10–30 min

    Take a timed diagnostic module; start the Error Log

    Reflect5–10 min

    Log what you missed and why in your Error Log.

  2. Module 2

    Reading & Writing Strategy

    Hook5–10 min

    One short passage, one question: read the question first

    Rep10–30 min

    Timed R&W practice set; log misses by type

    Reflect5–10 min

    Log what you missed and why in your Error Log.

  3. Module 3

    Math Strategy + Desmos

    Hook5–10 min

    Solve one problem by hand and with Desmos, compare speed

    Rep10–30 min

    Timed Math practice set using Desmos where it saves time

    Reflect5–10 min

    Log what you missed and why in your Error Log.

  4. Module 4

    Pacing Per Module

    Hook5–10 min

    Module 1 performance shapes Module 2, so don't rush the easy ones

    Rep10–30 min

    Pacing drill: flag, skip, return; two timed modules

    Reflect5–10 min

    Log what you missed and why in your Error Log.

  5. Module 5

    Test-Day Nerves

    Hook5–10 min

    Link to Course 6 Reset Rep: breathing and the 60-second reset

    Rep10–30 min

    Full practice test under real conditions

    Reflect5–10 min

    Log what you missed and why in your Error Log.

  6. Module 6

    Personal Study Plan

    Hook5–10 min

    Review the Error Log: which 3 mistake types cost the most?

    Rep10–30 min

    Build a 6–8 week plan with weekly timed modules and review sessions

    Reflect5–10 min

    Log what you missed and why in your Error Log.

Real-World Rep

Register for a test date (or pick a target date) and complete two weeks of the study plan.
