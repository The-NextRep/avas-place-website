Knowing yourself

Confidence & Self-Worth

Confidence is built through reps, not born. Learners separate who they are from grades, likes and wins, and grow confidence through small, repeated acts of courage.

4 sessions · adapts to all four learner tiers (K–college)

Start session 1 Print a Rep Card

Core tool

The Evidence File

A running list of proof: things I tried, things I did even though I was nervous, times I kept going. When the inner critic talks, learners read their evidence.

Before you start: quick self-check

Rate each line from 1 (not yet) to 5 (definitely) before session 1, then again after your Real-World Rep.

I like who I am even when I lose or fail
I can speak up in a group

The sessions

4 sessions, three beats each.

Every session runs Hook → Rep → Reflect, so learners know what to expect each time.

  1. Session 1

    I Am More Than a Score

    Hook5–10 min

    Two students with the same grade: one says "I'm dumb," one says "I need a new plan"

    Rep10–30 min

    Write 5 "I am…" statements that have nothing to do with grades, looks or wins

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

  2. Session 2

    The Inner Critic

    Hook5–10 min

    Read an unkind inner voice out loud: would you say this to a friend?

    Rep10–30 min

    Rewrite 3 harsh self-talk lines as a coach would say them

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

  3. Session 3

    Brave Reps

    Hook5–10 min

    Confidence comes after action, not before

    Rep10–30 min

    Pick a small brave act (ask a question, say hi, raise a hand) and do it this week

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

  4. Session 4

    Speak Up

    Hook5–10 min

    Body language changes how you feel and how others hear you

    Rep10–30 min

    Give a 30-second to 2-minute talk (by tier) on something you care about

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

Real-World Rep

Complete 3 brave reps this week and add each one to your Evidence File.

By age

How it adapts for each learner tier.

K–5

Sprouts

20 min sessions

"I Can" jar filled with notes of things they've learned to do. Show-and-tell with a strong voice and eye contact.

6–8

Builders

30 min sessions

Social media comparison check: what's real vs. edited. Build an Evidence File and a personal "confidence playlist" of reminders.

9–12

Launchers

40 min sessions

Identity map (values, strengths, interests) separate from sports stats, GPA or followers. Practice a mock interview introduction.

College

Leaders

50 min or self-paced sessions

Imposter-feeling lab for first-gen and new students: name it, gather evidence, speak in class or office hours once this week. Draft a personal introduction for networking and interviews.
