Schools, districts & colleges

Bring Ava's Place to your students.

All 21 courses are free for families. Schools and colleges pay one flat yearly fee for the training, support and reporting around them, and Title I schools get the full package free through sponsors.

Title I schools

Free for the schools that need it most.

Each sponsor covers the full license for one or more Title I schools for a year, about $1,000 per school. Sponsors are named on those schools' course pages and in our annual impact report.

Title I schools can apply with the form below. Businesses and donors who want to sponsor a school can use it too.
