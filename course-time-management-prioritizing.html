Managing yourself

Time Management & Prioritizing

You can't do everything, so choose on purpose. Learners sort tasks by importance, protect focus time and plan around their real energy.

4 sessions · adapts to all four learner tiers (K–college)

Start session 1 Print a Rep Card

Core tool

The Big Rocks priority sort (Must / Should / Could), and the Energy Ledger, a daily check of school load, social load and physical tiredness before planning.

Before you start: quick self-check

Rate each line from 1 (not yet) to 5 (definitely) before session 1, then again after your Real-World Rep.

I know what matters most this week
I can focus without my phone

The sessions

4 sessions, three beats each.

Every session runs Hook → Rep → Reflect, so learners know what to expect each time.

  1. Session 1

    Big Rocks First

    Hook5–10 min

    Jar demo: rocks, pebbles, sand; order changes what fits

    Rep10–30 min

    Sort a week of real tasks into Must / Should / Could

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

  2. Session 2

    Where Did the Time Go?

    Hook5–10 min

    Guess vs. actual: how long do common tasks really take?

    Rep10–30 min

    Time-log one afternoon; compare guesses to reality

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

  3. Session 3

    The Phone Problem

    Hook5–10 min

    Count notifications during a 10-minute task

    Rep10–30 min

    Try a phone-away focus sprint (10–25 min by tier) and compare output

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

  4. Session 4

    Plan Backward

    Hook5–10 min

    Show a project due in two weeks and the panic the night before

    Rep10–30 min

    Break one assignment into dated steps on a calendar

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

Real-World Rep

Run one week with a Big Rocks list each morning. Count how many Musts got done.

By age

How it adapts for each learner tier.

K–5

Sprouts

20 min sessions

Picture schedule and "first-then" boards. Sand-timer races to feel how long 5 minutes is.

6–8

Builders

30 min sessions

Paper planner setup, homework-before-screens routine, and a 10-minute focus sprint.

9–12

Launchers

40 min sessions

Balance school, a job, sports and family. Build a weekly plan with the Energy Ledger; 20-minute focus sprints.

College

Leaders

50 min or self-paced sessions

Syllabus-to-semester map: every deadline from every class on one calendar. Build a weekly schedule with class, work, study blocks and rest. 25-minute focus sprints.
