Built for every age

One catalog, four learner tiers.

Every course adapts its stories, practice and session length to where a learner is.

K–5

Sprouts

20-minute sessions

Stories, games, movement and picture cards.

Grades 6–8

Builders

30-minute sessions

Scenarios, pair work and simple trackers.

Grades 9–12

Launchers

40-minute sessions

Case studies, role-play and personal plans.

College & first-year adults

Leaders

50 minutes or self-paced

Real-life projects, peer coaching and reflection writing.

How a session works

Three beats, every time.

Hook

Surface the problem

A story, scenario or quick game that brings the challenge to life.

Rep

Practice the skill

A low-stakes practice round. A little discomfort on purpose, in a safe setting.

Reflect

Log what happened

One line on the Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

Every course ends with a Real-World Rep: one task learners complete outside the session, then report back on. Growth is tracked with simple before-and-after self-checks. No grades, no tests.

Our mission

Every student deserves a place to build real-world confidence.

Ava's Place Foundation builds the skills students most often lack when school stops handing them a script: handling uncertainty, taking feedback, managing time, bouncing back and making their own choices.

Our courses are free for every family, and every Bella book sold supports Fairfax County Public Schools.
