Managing yourself

Self-Direction & Initiative

Nobody is going to tell you every step, and that's your chance. Learners set their own goals, start without being told and hold themselves accountable.

4 sessions · adapts to all four learner tiers (K–college)

Start session 1 Print a Rep Card

Core tool

The Own-It Loop

Choose a goal · Set the first step · Start before you feel ready · Check in with yourself · Adjust.

Before you start: quick self-check

Rate each line from 1 (not yet) to 5 (definitely) before session 1, then again after your Real-World Rep.

I start things without reminders
I check my own progress

The sessions

4 sessions, three beats each.

Every session runs Hook → Rep → Reflect, so learners know what to expect each time.

  1. Session 1

    Waiting vs. Starting

    Hook5–10 min

    Two students, same assignment: one waits for reminders, one starts

    Rep10–30 min

    List three things they usually wait to be told to do

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

  2. Session 2

    My Why

    Hook5–10 min

    Short story of someone driven by a personal reason

    Rep10–30 min

    Write a personal "why" for one goal

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

  3. Session 3

    The 2-Minute Start

    Hook5–10 min

    Show how starting is the hardest part

    Rep10–30 min

    Pick a stalled task and do only the first 2 minutes

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

  4. Session 4

    Be Your Own Coach

    Hook5–10 min

    Compare a coach checking in vs. no one checking

    Rep10–30 min

    Set up a self-check-in routine and an accountability buddy

    Reflect5–10 min

    Write one line on your Rep Card: what I tried, what happened, what I'll try next.

Real-World Rep

Start one thing this week before anyone reminds you. Log when you started and what happened.

By age

How it adapts for each learner tier.

K–5

Sprouts

20 min sessions

Classroom "helper who notices" game: spot something that needs doing and do it without being asked. Sticker chart for self-started jobs.

6–8

Builders

30 min sessions

Choose-your-own mini project (two weeks) with a self-made checklist and check-in dates.

9–12

Launchers

40 min sessions

Personal passion project or job/college application plan with milestones they set and track themselves.

College

Leaders

50 min or self-paced sessions

"No one is taking attendance" lab: design a personal semester system (study hours, office hours visits, campus resources). Pair up as accountability partners with weekly check-ins.
