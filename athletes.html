Aspiring Athlete Program

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't.

Many young athletes have the drive and the ability but not the money for training, showcases or a stable place to live. The Aspiring Athlete Program closes that gap, so they can show what they can do.

Give to the program Apply for support

Why it matters

The next level costs money most families don't have.

Getting seen by scouts and recruiters takes training, coaching, travel and showcase entry. For athletes between programs or far from home, it can also mean finding a safe, stable place to live.

Ava's Place believes no athlete should miss their shot because of where they started. The program gives them the support to get ready and the life skills to succeed after the game.

What the program covers

Support on and off the field.

Training & coaching

Strength, speed and position training, plus recovery, so athletes show up ready.

Showcase access

Entry fees and travel for combines and showcases in front of pro and college scouts.

Stable housing

Help with a safe place to live and train while an athlete prepares.

Equipment & life skills

Gear, plus free Ava's Place courses on NIL, money, mindset and the career beyond sports.

How it works

One program, many athletes.

Step 1

You give to the program

Your gift goes into the Aspiring Athlete Program at Ava's Place Foundation, not to any one person.

Step 2

Athletes apply

Athletes, or a coach or family member on their behalf, apply and share what they need to reach the next level.

Step 3

The foundation decides

Ava's Place reviews each request using written criteria, including financial need and readiness, and decides who receives support.

Apply for support

Are you an athlete who needs a boost?

High school, college and post-college athletes chasing the next level can apply. Coaches and family members can apply on an athlete's behalf.

Email us your name, sport and position, where you're training or competing next, and what support would help most.
