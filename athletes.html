Our promise

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't. No young athlete should miss their shot because of where they started, what their family can afford or where they sleep at night.

What the program covers

Everything it takes to get in the game.

Training & coaching

Strength, speed and skills training with real coaches, plus the recovery to stay healthy.

Showcase access

Entry fees and travel for camps, combines and showcases where scouts and recruiters are watching.

A stable place to grow

Help with safe, steady housing so athletes can focus on school, training and their future.

Gear & life skills

The equipment to compete, plus free Ava's Place courses on NIL, money, mindset and life after sports.

Who it's for

Every sport. Every level. Every kid with a dream.

Middle school athletesGrades 6–8 building the foundation
High school athletesGrades 9–12 chasing a roster spot or scholarship
College athletesWorking toward the next level
Any sportFootball, basketball, soccer, track, volleyball, baseball and more

How it works

From sign-up to game day.

01

Register

A parent or guardian registers their athlete and tells us their goals and what would help most.

02

Connect

We review each registration and talk with the family, and the coach when there is one.

03

Get support

Ava's Place decides who receives support using written criteria, including need, readiness and commitment.

04

Rise

Athletes train, compete and keep growing with life-skills courses and check-ins along the way.

For families

Register your athlete.
